---
title: "Discontent with Content"
date: 2026-11-22
---

I allowed myself to be content for the first time in recent memory the other night. The autobiographical minutia of the circumstances are irrelevant for purposes of this post, but let me simply share my heart was very full of love and hope.

However.

I woke up a few hours later with severe anxiety which lead me to learn about [allostatic loads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allostatic_load), so called "vulnerability hangovers," and how the body keeps the score even if the mind thinks it is several steps ahead.

I spent the next six hours essentially doom spiraling in bed trying to let drone music and benzodiazepines do their jobs. They really didn't.

The epiphany was simply that I am very wounded right now. I'm reading this book on grief. It talks about how grief is a very misunderstood concept in the west. We see it as something that ought to be "cured" or "solved" but the truth is perhaps closer to being "tended to" or "cared for."

Just as you cannot make a laceration heal faster you cannot make grief heal faster. It is on it's own timeline.

I've also heard that grief is a spiral.

I then realized am discontent with contentment. I'm skeptical of joy.

Being content is not something I allow myself to feel very often. I think this is largely because I have a suspicion that I will become complacent, fall asleep at the wheel, and then before I know it my life will be over. A siren song.

That's not true, and it isn't fair to me to live that sort of life.

I think humans fundamentally want the same things. To feel safe, to have their basic needs met, to have a warm place to sleep at night, to be loved. Sadly about a quarter fo the world's population doesn't have clean water. Isn't that wild?

I haven't lived a particularly traumatic life. It's been quite good on many of the axes that count. But I do hold deep wounds and scars that I'm tending to right now. This is a time of great transformation for me. I don't know what is next but my hope is that it will have a very different _texture_ than previous epochs.

I once told a dear friend that I relate strongly to a shark. Their biology is such that if they stop swimming water stops moving through their gills and they'll die. For most of my adult life I've felt the same. I need to keep moving forward or I will perish.

But.

I allowed myself to be content, despite the mess, the chaos, the pain.

And I didn't die.

There's something to learn there. I don't know what it is yet.

But I'll find out.
